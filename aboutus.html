About Garden Crowns

Garden Crowns, LLC is a small business located in Northwest Indiana. The concept was created by two sisters who enjoy creating fun memories and placing smiles on faces by offering a huge selection of yard signs at an affordable rental price.

Our personalized yard sign designs are sure to provide an experience that you and your loved ones will cherish forever for events such as birthdays, graduations, weddings, anniversaries, prom, baby announcements/showers, retirements, business announcements, and much more.

We are committed to placing our “Garden Crowns” in your yard to create an unforgettable memory that will last for many years to come. We currently serve Northwest Indiana and surrounding areas.
