About Garden Crowns

Garden Crowns, LLC is a small business located in Northwest Indiana. We specialize in creating personalized yard sign designs that help families, schools, businesses, and communities celebrate life’s most meaningful moments.

The concept was created by two sisters born and raised in East Chicago, Indiana. Being only 11 months apart, we have always shared a special bond as sisters, best friends, and lifelong supporters of one another.

We had the blessing of welcoming our first children around the same time, and while we were both on maternity leave, Garden Crowns began to blossom. What started as a creative idea quickly grew into something much bigger — a dream to build a legacy for our children while bringing joy to families throughout our community.

Our personalized yard sign designs are created to provide an experience that you and your loved ones will cherish forever. From birthdays, graduations, weddings, anniversaries, prom, baby announcements, retirements, business announcements, school events, indoor celebrations, and more, we love helping every moment feel extra special.

We are proud to be rooted in Northwest Indiana and committed to placing our “Garden Crowns” in your yard or event space to create unforgettable memories that will last for years to come.
